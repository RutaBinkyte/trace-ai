TRACE-AI 2026

Transparency, Provenance, Accountability, and Governance
for AI (Co-)Generated Content

Draft — all dates and speakers tentative

About

TRACE-AI focuses on the technical and governance infrastructure required to ensure AI-generated content and knowledge is trustworthy. As AI systems increasingly produce scientific manuscripts, generative art, legal documents, medical reports, and public information, the field needs mechanisms that can answer: how was this artifact produced, by whom, using what AI systems, with what inputs, and who is accountable for its claims? The workshop addresses the provenance, reproducibility, and auditability of AI contributions across the scientific and artistic pipeline, and the governance frameworks needed to operationalise accountability at scale.
